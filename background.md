# My Fin Tracker

## Background
Currently I manage my personal finance and more on a exact, you know the using excel in phone is cumbersome. Want as I am not expert in ms excel, formula can not created perfectly to see the real time calculation. To understand current situation I update monthly. Therefore I want to create a mobile app.

## Features
1. Monthly Saving
2. Saving for Something
3. Borrowed Management
4. Monthly Expenses
5. Dashboard
6. Investment
7. Buying
8. Spended

### Monthly Saving
- Monthly Savings records
- Expended from it
- Current Balance

### Saving for Something
I set a target for an item, then save for that item.
It should give me all current Saving in this section
Item wise save history
etc etc

### Borrowed Management
I can able to borrow from Monthly Savings or Saving for Something's item saving, also will add fine/charge money will be added to that field.
Default charge will be 5% of borrowed amount

### Monthly Expenses
I spend a fixed portion of my income, Break it down to category
eg: Fixed (sub category: House Rent, Gas bill, Electric bill etc), Ration, Random, Mine
Each category can have sub category or not

### Dashboard
Incomes: Salary, Bonus
Monthly Save, Carried Forward from last month left over
Month Opening data Bank Balance. 
All summed up and should be consistence with current balance etc

### Buying
Major thing I buy, Want it to be listed. and it is for the anual report

### In Cash
How much I have in cash in drawer for which sector etc

### Spended
For a thing, like long term project, how much I spended on this

### Count Down
I want to track a budget, I how spended already
