My Fin Tracker

My Fin Tracker is a personal finance app for Android, made for managing money in Bangladeshi taka (৳). It tracks spending against a monthly plan, savings pots and goals, loans, income tax and zakat.

Your data stays on your phone. There is no account and no server. If you choose, the app can keep an encrypted backup in your own Google Drive.
