1The main ideas

The app answers two questions about every taka:

  • Where it is: your accounts, such as Bank, Cash and bKash.
  • What it's for: your pots. Spending is this month's money. Monthly Savings is savings. Goals are things you are saving for, like a laptop.

The same ৳10,000 in the bank can be split between Spending and Savings. Both cards on Home always add up to the same total.

Left to spendWhat is still in the Spending pot this month.
BudgetedThe part of it that your category budgets still need.
UnplannedMoney no budget is waiting for. You can use it freely.
ShortBudgets need more than you have left, usually because a category went over.

Months run from the 1st to the last day. Amounts are in taka with lakh grouping (৳1,23,456). Balances are never typed in. They are always worked out from your entries, so the app stays Balanced.

2First-time setup

  1. Accounts & pots (More → Accounts & pots): add your banks, cash and wallets. Add savings pots and goals. A goal can have a target amount and date.
  2. Categories (More → Categories): expense sections with subsections, such as Ration → Rice Bag. Mark rent and bills as fixed and give them a due day. A category can also send its unused budget to a pot when the month closes.
  3. Starting balances (More → Starting balances): enter what each account holds today. You can split it between Spending and Savings. Enter balances before any expenses. Entries dated earlier than the starting balances get counted twice.
  4. Monthly plan (More → Monthly plan): your income, how it is split, and the budget for each category. Section 8 explains this.
Want to look around first? More → Example data → Load example fills the app with two sample months. It replaces everything, so only use it before you enter real data.

Tap any entry to see its details, edit it, add a receipt photo or delete it. More → All entries lists everything by month.

4Adding entries every day

Tap +. Choose Expense, Income or Move, type the amount on the number pad, pick a category and save. The app remembers the last account you used.

Expense

  • Paid from: which account and pot it came out of (usually Cash or Bank · Spending).
  • Receipt photo: tap the camera icon in the note field and take a photo or choose one. Tap it again to remove it before saving.
  • Major purchase: also adds it to the Buying list, where you can keep the shop and warranty.
  • Tracker chips count the expense towards a project or countdown, such as a trip.

Income

Choose the income type and the account it arrived in. The For November chip holds money received now for next month (see section 8).

Move

Money changing place or purpose without being spent. Examples: withdraw from bank (Bank · Spending → Cash · Spending), or putting money into a goal.

Faster: long-press the app icon for Add expense, or add the home-screen widget. The widget shows what's left to spend and has its own + button.

5Bank and bKash messages

The app can read your City Bank and bKash text messages and offer each payment as an entry. It reads them on the phone only and never sends them anywhere.

  1. Turn it on in More → Rules → Suggest entries from SMS and allow SMS access. Messages from the 1st of this month onward are read.
  2. Home → Needs attention shows "3 bank messages to add". Tap it.
  3. Add opens Quick add with the amount, date, account and shop filled in. Choose a category and save. Dismiss hides a message for good.
  • A bKash cash out or an ATM withdrawal is offered as a move to Cash, with the fee in the note.
  • Maybe already added means an entry of the same amount already exists within a day.
  • Senders (at the bottom of the messages screen) sets which senders to read and which account each one is. Add others, such as Nagad, by name or number.
Not read? OTPs, offers and failed transactions are skipped on purpose. If a real payment is missed or read wrongly, note the message with account numbers hidden so the reader can be improved.

6Home

  • Close September appears after a month ends (see section 12).
  • Needs attention lists bank messages to add, income that should have arrived, bills due and slots planned in the next 3 days, overdue or long loans, investments maturing soon, and an overdue backup. Tap one to go to the right screen.
  • Ready for November shows income held for next month.
  • Left to spend is the big card, with about how much a day you can spend.
  • This month shows how you got to that number: carried forward, income, set aside, spent, loans.
  • Where it is / What it's for shows balances by account and by pot. Match balance and Cash drawer are here too.
  • Net worth includes investments and loans.

Tap a section's heading to fold it away. Folded, it shows just its total or count, and stays folded next time.

Two Homes

Choose in More → Appearance → Home page:

  • Standard: the big number is everything left to spend, bills included.
  • After bills: the big number is Free to spend. Fixed bills still to pay, and money kept in slots, are taken off, and the sum is shown in the card (Left to spend − bills still to pay = free to spend). A Bills to pay card lists each unpaid bill with its due date, and Pay all.

7Expenses and budgets

The Spend tab shows each category's spending against its budget. Orange means close to the budget and red means over it. Fixed bills show due 15 Oct or paid. Pay remaining fixed bills records them all at once.

Compared with last month

Under a category that changed a lot you'll see ↑ 25% vs Sep (spending more) or ↓ (spending less). The Compared with September card shows the totals and the biggest changes. During a month it compares the same days, for example 1–14 Oct against 1–14 Sep, so a half month isn't set against a full one. It needs a previous month with expenses. Turn it off in More → Rules → Compare with last month.

Slots and budget moves (this month only)

  • New slot: set money aside for something coming this month, such as a medical test. The money is taken from other categories. Unused money can be given back. Add the date you're likely to spend it, now or later with Set date. From 3 days before, it shows on Home under Needs attention until its money is spent.
  • Move budget: move budget from one category to another for this month only.

Neither changes your Monthly plan. Next month starts from the plan again.

Save to a pot during the month

Save to pot moves part of a category's budget into a savings pot or goal now, instead of waiting for month end. Example: Rice Bag's ৳1,000 into a Rice Bag pot each month. You can also tap a category that is linked to a pot. This month's budget for the category drops and the money moves out of Spending, so nothing shows as Short. A fixed bill saved in full no longer shows as due, and closing the month won't save it again. Undo in the same card reverses it.

8Salary and the monthly plan

More → Monthly plan has three tabs:

  • Income: each regular income with its amount, usual day and account. The app reminds you if it hasn't arrived.
  • Split: what to set aside when the salary arrives, to savings, goals or a DPS.
  • Expenses: the budget for each category.

Salary on the 26th for next month

If your salary arrives near the end of a month and you spend it the next month, turn on For next month for that income. When you record it, it's held apart and shown as Ready for November. On the 1st it becomes spending money, and your split runs then.

9Savings, goals and loans

Savings and goals

A pot such as Monthly Savings holds money for a purpose. A goal is a pot with a target. The goal screen shows progress, whether you are on track and the monthly amount needed. Buy it records the purchase and closes the goal.

Borrowing from your own savings

When the month runs short you can borrow from your savings. The loan has a charge, 5% by default (change it in More → Rules), so borrowing has a cost and you pay it back.

Loans with people

More → Loans → People: money you lent or borrowed, with an optional charge and due date. Record repayments as they come. Overdue and long-open loans show on Home. For a loan that started before you used the app, use Started before I used the app.

10Cash and matching balances

  • Cash drawer (More → Cash drawer): cash split by pot, plus Count cash by notes and coins.
  • Match balance (Home): type what your bank app or wallet really shows. The app records the difference as an adjustment, so it matches reality.

11Investments, buying, trackers

  • Investments: DPS, FDR, savings certificates and shares, with deposits, value updates and maturity reminders.
  • Buying: major purchases with shop and warranty end date.
  • Trackers: a Project (total spent on a house repair, say) or a Countdown (a set budget for a trip, showing what's left).

12Closing a month

After the 1st, Home shows Close September. The close screen:

  1. Asks you to check the bank balance against the app.
  2. Offers to save each category's unused budget to the pot linked to it.
  3. Lets you split the leftover: carry it forward, or send parts to savings, goals or pots.
  4. Returns unused slot money.

The default choice for the leftover is set in More → Rules → Leftover at month end.

13Tools: afford, zakat, tax

All three are in More → Tools.

Can I afford it?

Type a price. The answer is one of four:

AnswerMeaning
YesIt fits in unplanned money.
Yes, but it is tightIt uses money your budgets still need.
Not from this month alonePart must come from savings. The borrowing charge is shown.
Not nowIt's more than this month's money and your savings together.

You also see what's left a day afterwards and how many months saving up would take. Save up for it as a goal creates the goal.

Zakat

Bank, cash, wallets, investments and money owed to you come from the app. Debts you owe people are taken off. Add gold and silver by weight and today's price a gram (1 bhori ≈ 11.66 g). Then choose the silver or gold nisab. If your net wealth is over the nisab, zakat is 2.5% of it.

Typing over a filled-in figure keeps your number. The ↺ button brings the app's figure back.

Get today's price fills in the international price of pure gold and silver a gram, in taka. It's the only thing in the app that uses the internet, and only when you tap it. Jewellers' prices (BAJUS) are usually higher, so type those instead if you prefer.

Zakat date: choose the day your zakat is due. Once it passes, the same date next year is used. The app then shows the zakat today and the expected zakat on that date. The expected figure assumes your wealth keeps growing by your average of the last three months, or by an amount you type, at today's prices.

Income tax (Bangladesh, simplified)

An estimate for the income year July to June. Salary is what you received so far plus your monthly plan for the months still to come. Every step is shown:

  1. Total income minus the salary exemption (one third of salary, at most ৳5,00,000) gives taxable income.
  2. The slabs are applied: first ৳4,00,000 at 0%, next ৳3,00,000 at 10%, next ৳4,00,000 at 15%, next ৳5,00,000 at 20%, next ৳20,00,000 at 25%, the rest at 30%.
  3. The investment rebate is taken off (DPS, savings certificates, insurance, PF, listed shares).
  4. The minimum tax (৳5,000) applies if your income is over the tax-free limit.
  5. Tax already deducted is taken off, leaving still to pay or a refund.

While the year is running you see two figures. Full year (to 30 June) uses your plan for the months still to come. Up to today is the tax on the income your entries show so far, as if the year ended today. Switch between them to see the steps for each.

Choose your taxpayer type (general; woman or 65+; disability; freedom fighter) for the right tax-free limit. Tap the settings button at the top to change slabs and rates when a new Finance Act changes them.

Estimates only. Zakat depends on your circumstances, so ask a scholar. Tax rates change most years, so check with NBR or a tax adviser before filing.

14Reports and export

More → Reports & export:

  • Monthly report (PDF): income, where it went, expenses against budget, comparison with last month, savings, goals, loans and every entry.
  • Annual report (PDF): the year so far, month by month.
  • Export for Excel (CSV): every entry. Opens in Excel with Bangla text intact.

Each one can be shared, printed or saved to the phone.

15Backup and a new phone

  • More → Backup & restore: Save to phone or Share a .mft backup file, for example to Google Drive or email. It includes your receipt photos.
  • Automatic backup makes one a week and keeps the last 8 in the app's folder. Those copies are lost if the app is uninstalled, so share one off the phone now and then.
  • New phone: install the app, then Backup & restore → Restore from file. Restoring replaces everything in the app.
Never uninstall the app to update it. Install the new version over the old one, and your data stays.

16Settings and security

  • App lock (More → Rules): fingerprint or phone PIN. The app asks again after 1 minute away. The widget then shows Locked.
  • Rules: borrowing charge, leftover at month end, SMS suggestions, compare with last month.
  • Appearance: System, Light or Dark; three colours; Pitch black for OLED screens.
  • About: version and open-source licences.

17Questions and problems

Home says "Short ৳3,500". Am I out of money?
No. Your budgets need more than is left in Spending, usually because one category (such as Ration) went over. Lower a budget for this month with Move budget, or accept that less is left for the others.
A total looks too high or counted twice.
Check for expenses dated before your starting balances. The starting balance already includes them, so they count twice. Change their date or delete them.
The bank app shows a different balance.
Use Match balance on Home. If the difference is a payment you forgot, add that entry instead.
"Not balanced" appears at the top of Home.
This shouldn't happen. Make a backup and report it.
A receipt says "Photo not found".
The photo file is missing, for example after restoring a backup made before receipts existed. Use Replace to add it again.
I don't see the comparison with last month.
It appears once there's a previous month with expenses, and only when it's switched on in Rules.
Bank messages don't appear.
Check that SMS suggestions are on and SMS access is allowed in Android settings. Also check that the sender's name matches one in Senders. Only messages from the start date onward are read.
