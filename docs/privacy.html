Privacy policy

My Fin Tracker

Last updated: 9 November 2026

My Fin Tracker ("the app") is made by Rohan BK. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your financial data stays on your phone, and the developer never receives it.

Data kept on your phone

Everything you enter (accounts, entries, budgets, savings, loans, receipt photos and settings) is stored only on your device. The developer has no server and cannot see, collect or share this data.

Bank text messages

If you allow it, the app reads bank and mobile-wallet text messages on your phone to suggest entries. They are read on the device only and are never sent anywhere.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on Google Drive backup, the app asks for permission to use one scope: drive.appdata. This lets it create, read and delete its own backup files in a hidden app folder of your Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

  • Backups are encrypted on your phone with a password you choose (AES-256) before they are uploaded. Without the password they cannot be opened, by Google or by the developer.
  • The password is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • The files go only to your own Google account. The developer has no access to them.
  • You can stop at any time with Turn off in the app, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the backups, go to Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → My Fin Tracker → Delete hidden app data.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other internet use

For the zakat calculator, the app can download today's public gold and silver prices. No personal information is sent with that request.

Sharing and selling

The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone. It has no ads and no analytics.

Deleting your data

Use More → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall it. Remove Drive backups as described above.

Changes and contact
