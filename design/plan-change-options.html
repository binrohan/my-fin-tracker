Changing the plan from next month

Three ways the Monthly plan could handle a change that starts next month. Pick one; the behaviour behind it (October keeps its plan, November uses the new one) is the same in all three, except "Revert".

Your case: the salary goes up from ৳85,000 to ৳1,00,000. The raised salary arrives on 26 October and is spent in November. October's budgets and split must not change; November's should.

A. Ask when saving

Recommended

No tabs. You edit the plan exactly as today. When you tap Save, the app asks when it should apply. A change waiting for next month shows as a note at the top.

Good

  • Screen looks like today
  • The question comes at the moment it matters
  • Waiting change is always visible

Watch out

  • One extra tap on every Save

B. Separate button

Alternative

The plan always opens on this month. A button at the bottom opens the same screen in a clearly coloured November plan mode.

Good

  • Normal saves have no extra question
  • Hard to change the wrong month: the bar is always visible

Watch out

  • You have to know to use the button before editing
  • Two places that look alike

C. Revert

Back to before

One plan, as before 1.2.0. Changes apply from the current month. For the raise you would wait and edit on 1 November, after the salary is released and before you spend.

Good

  • Simplest screen

Watch out

  • The 1 November split uses the old plan unless you edit on the 26th–31st
  • Budgets edited in October change October
  • Tax prices earlier months at the new salary
  • The 26 October reminder expects the old amount
