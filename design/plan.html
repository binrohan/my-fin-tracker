My Fin Tracker

A personal finance app for your Android phone that replaces the monthly Excel sheet. Every total is calculated from the entries you add, so nothing needs a formula and the numbers always agree with each other. All data stays on the phone, and a single backup file moves it to a new phone.

The core idea

Every taka has a place and a purpose

The Excel sheet is hard to keep consistent because each total is typed or built with its own formula. In the app you only record events: income, expenses and moves. Every balance is then a sum of those events, so the totals can't disagree.

Every entry is stored as a few postings. Each posting is a pair (account, fund) with a plus or minus amount. That one table answers everything you asked for:

  • Bank / cash balance is the sum of postings by account.
  • Monthly Savings, each goal, Spending is the sum of postings by fund.
  • In Cash by sector is the Cash account broken down by fund.
  • Carried forward is the Spending fund's balance on the first day of the month. Nothing needs to be copied by hand.
  • Consistency check: the account totals always equal the fund totals. You compare the Bank figure with your bank app, and any difference shows up straight away.

Your list, specified

Features and the rules behind them

The tag on each card shows the phase it is built in. "Spended" and "Count Down" use the same mechanism (tag expenses, add them up), so they share one screen called Trackers.

Dashboard

Phase 2

Shows the current month: the month's opening figures, income, where the money went and what's left.

  • Opening: bank, cash and wallet balances on day 1, plus the amount carried forward.
  • Income: Salary, Bonus and other types you configure.
  • Left to spend = carried forward + income − set aside − spent ± borrow/repay.
  • "Balanced ✓" chip. Tap "Match bank" to enter your bank app's balance. Any difference becomes a labelled adjustment.
  • Net worth, including investments.

Monthly Expenses

Phase 2

A category tree with an optional budget for each category, every month.

  • Categories with optional sub-categories, e.g. Fixed › House Rent, Gas, Electricity; Ration; Random; Mine.
  • Budget as a fixed amount or a % of income, copied forward each month.
  • Fixed bills are recurring: one tap pays all the bills due.
  • Colour states: on track, near limit (≥ 90%), over.

Quick add

Phase 2

An expense in three taps: amount, category, save.

  • Opens on the number pad. The last-used account and fund are preselected.
  • Your most-used categories are shown as chips.
  • Optional: note, date, "major purchase", tracker tag.
  • Launcher shortcut and home-screen widget "+ Expense" (Phase 6).

Monthly Saving

Phase 3

The main savings pot. Each month's deposit, every withdrawal, and the running balance.

  • Suggested deposit when salary arrives, from your allocation plan.
  • Withdraw (spent for good) or Borrow (to be paid back with a charge).
  • History by month, and the amount still owed back to it.

Saving for Something

Phase 3

One goal per item: a target amount, an optional target date and a deposit history.

  • Progress %, and the monthly amount needed = (target − saved) ÷ months left.
  • On-track / behind chip, with the projected finish date at your current rate.
  • "Buy it" spends from the goal, closes it and adds the item to Buying.
  • Overview: the total across all goals.

Borrowed Management

Phase 3

Two kinds of loan: borrowing from your own savings, and lending to or borrowing from other people.

  • Own savings: borrow from Monthly Savings or any goal. Charge = amount × rate, default 5%, changeable in Settings and for each loan. Each repayment goes back to the fund it came from.
  • People: money you lent to someone, or borrowed from someone. Each person has a running balance: they owe you, or you owe them. Charge is optional (default 0%).
  • Part payments, a due date, a reminder when a loan is overdue or open for 60+ days.
  • Net worth counts what people owe you and what you owe them.

In Cash

Phase 3

How much is in the drawer, and which sector each part belongs to.

  • Breakdown by fund comes from the postings automatically.
  • "Count cash": a note-by-note counter (1000, 500, 200…) compared with the app's figure.
  • Move money between sectors, or deposit it to the bank.

Investment

Phase 4

DPS, FDR, savings certificates, stocks: anything that isn't spendable cash.

  • Each investment is its own account: principal, start date, rate, maturity.
  • Instalments are transfers. Update the current value whenever you check it.
  • Maturity reminders. Returns are tracked separately from salary.

Buying

Phase 4

A register of major purchases for the annual report.

  • Turn on "major purchase" on any expense, or use a goal's "Buy it".
  • Item, price, shop, warranty end date, optional receipt photo.
  • Yearly list and total. Warranty expiry reminders.

Trackers: Spended + Count Down

Phase 4

Tag expenses to a long-term project or to a budget with an end date.

  • Project (Spended): lifetime total, by month and by category, no limit.
  • Countdown: budget − spent = left, plus days to go.
  • Tagging doesn't change which month or category an expense counts under.

Monthly PDF report

Phase 5

Made on the phone when you close a month, or whenever you ask. You can share, save or print it.

  • Summary, reconciliation, income, budget vs actual, savings, goals, loans, cash, investments, purchases, trackers, full ledger.
  • Annual report: month-by-month totals, savings growth, purchase register.

Configurable

Phase 1+

Everything you named is a setting, not code.

  • Categories, sub-categories, budgets, accounts, funds, income types.
  • Borrow charge %, month start day (e.g. salary day), currency and number grouping (1,00,000 or 100,000).
  • What happens to month-end leftover: carry forward, or move to savings.
  • App lock (fingerprint), light/dark theme.
Design

Screen designs

Shown as of 7 Oct 2026 with sample data. Nine screens. Four tabs (Home, Spend, Save, More) and a central + button for quick add. The mockups follow your light or dark setting, as the app will.

How it's built

Tech stack and architecture

PartChoiceWhy
App frameworkFlutter (Dart)Builds an Android APK on your Ubuntu machine with no cloud build service. It also runs as a Linux desktop window, which makes trying out changes fast.
DatabaseSQLite via DriftThe data is one file on the phone. Drift gives typed queries and safe schema upgrades, and its live queries update the screens as soon as you add an entry.
State & navigationRiverpod, go_routerStandard, well-tested Flutter packages.
PDFpdf + printingBuilds the PDF on the phone, then opens Android's share, save or print sheet.

Why not something else

  • React Native / Expo: also workable, but building an APK without Expo's cloud still needs the same Android SDK setup, and on-device PDF is weaker.
  • Web app (PWA): the browser can clear its storage, which is not acceptable for financial records.
  • iPhone: not needed. The app is Android only, as you confirmed.
Under the hood

Data model and formulas

Twelve tables. No balance is stored. Every balance is calculated from the postings, which is why the numbers can't drift apart.

TableHoldsKey fields
accountsWhere money isname, type (bank / cash / wallet / investment / person), archived. A person account above zero means they owe you; below zero means you owe them.
fundsWhat money is forname, kind (spending / savings / goal / investment), target, target date, achieved
categoriesExpense & income treeparent (optional), name, kind, is_fixed, due day, icon
entriesOne user actiondate, type (income / expense / move / borrow / repay / adjust), category, note, tracker, loan
postingsIts effectsentry, account, fund, amount (signed, poisha)
periodsMonthsstart, end, closed at, bank figure you confirmed
budgetsPlan per categoryperiod, category, amount or % of income
loansBorrowing & lendingkind (own savings / lent to person / borrowed from person), person, fund, principal, charge rate, charge, date, due date, status
investmentsAsset detailsaccount, kind, rate, maturity, instalment, valuations
purchasesBuying registerentry, item, shop, warranty until, photo
trackersProjects & countdownsname, kind, budget (optional), start, end
settings, recurringConfigurationkey/value; recurring bill templates
FigureFormula
Account / fund balanceΣ postings for that account / fund up to the date
Left to spendSpending fund balance in bank, cash and wallets
Carried forwardSpending fund balance at the start of the period
Budget leftbudget − Σ expenses in the category this period
Loan due / leftprincipal × (1 + rate) / due − Σ repayments
Goal needs per month(target − saved) ÷ months to target date
Countdown leftbudget − Σ tagged expenses; days = end − today
Net worthΣ all accounts: spendable + investment values + owed to you − you owe
Balanced ✓Σ accounts = Σ funds (built in) and the bank figure = the balance you confirmed
Your data, your device

Backup and moving to a new phone

  • Backup file myfin-2026-10-04.mft is a zip containing the data as JSON, plus a manifest with the app version, schema version, record counts and a checksum.
  • Optional password encrypts the file with AES-GCM. Without the password, nobody can read it, including you.
  • Auto backup weekly (or daily) to a folder you pick on the phone. Keeps the last 8 copies.
  • No service used. You decide where the file goes: USB cable, Bluetooth, Nearby Share, or your own cloud drive if you choose.
  • CSV export of all entries, so you can open them in Excel at any time.
Moving to a new phone: install the APK → open the app → "Restore from file" → pick the .mft → enter the password if you set one. The app checks the checksum, upgrades older backups to the current schema, and shows record counts before replacing anything.
Phase 0

Setting up your machine from zero

I checked your machine: Ubuntu 24.04, Java 21, git, curl and VS Code are installed. Flutter, the Android SDK and adb are missing. I will run these steps for you. You only need to approve the sudo steps and plug in your phone. About 10 GB of disk; you have 98 GB free.

1. System packages (for Flutter's Linux desktop build and Android tools)

sudo apt update
sudo apt install -y clang cmake ninja-build pkg-config libgtk-3-dev liblzma-dev libstdc++-12-dev

2. Flutter SDK

git clone https://github.com/flutter/flutter.git -b stable ~/dev/flutter
echo 'export PATH="$HOME/dev/flutter/bin:$PATH"' >> ~/.bashrc
flutter --version

3. Android SDK (command-line tools only, no Android Studio)

# download commandlinetools-linux from developer.android.com into ~/Android/Sdk/cmdline-tools/latest
sdkmanager "platform-tools" "platforms;android-<latest>" "build-tools;<latest>"
flutter config --android-sdk ~/Android/Sdk
flutter doctor --android-licenses
flutter doctor

4. Your phone and VS Code

# Phone: Settings › About › tap "Build number" 7× › Developer options › USB debugging (or Wireless debugging)
adb devices                       # phone should be listed
code --install-extension Dart-Code.flutter
flutter create --org com.myfin --platforms android,linux my_fin_tracker
flutter run                       # hot-reloads on your phone as we build
Use your real phone rather than the Android emulator. Your machine had 25 of 30 GB RAM in use when I checked, and the emulator needs several more. Testing on the phone also shows exactly what you'll get. To install a finished version: flutter build apk --release, then copy the APK to the phone or run adb install.
Order of work

Roadmap

Each phase ends with an APK on your phone that you can use. Backup is in Phase 2 because your real data starts going in then. Durations assume a few focused sessions a week.

0½ day

Setup

Tools installed, empty app running on your phone with hot reload. Done when: "Hello" shows on the phone.

1~1 week

Foundation: the money engine

Database, accounts, funds, categories, entries → postings, periods, settings, number formatting, app shell with tabs. Unit tests for every formula. Done when: the September sample month above can be entered in tests and reproduces every figure in the report.

2~1–2 weeks

Quick add, Expenses with budgets and recurring fixed bills, Dashboard with Match bank, salary allocation plan, month close with carry forward, backup and restore. You start fresh from the 1st of a month (no Excel import). Done when: you stop updating the Excel sheet.
